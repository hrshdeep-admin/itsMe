The Reframe The ADHD diagnosis reframes everything — your "procrastination" and "planning-but-not-executing" pattern was never a character flaw. This plan is built on external structure, friction design, and small dopamine wins — not willpower.
Operating Rules

ADHD Operating Rules

These six apply to everything below. They are the terrain the whole plan is built on.

Rule 01

Never rely on willpower

Rely on environment: phone in another room, workout clothes laid out the night before.

Rule 02

25-min timer blocks

Pomodoro. Never "study for 1 hour" — always "do two 25-min blocks."

Rule 03

Visible tracking

Physical wall calendar, ❌ marked daily. Streaks = dopamine.

Rule 04

2-minute start rule

Commit only to starting; momentum does the rest.

Rule 05

One priority per day

Anything extra is bonus, not baseline.

Rule 06 · 🏥

Follow up with your doctor

ADHD treatment options (medication/coaching) — a Month 1 task, not optional.
