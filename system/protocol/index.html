04d · The Operating System

The Protocol Book

This is not a to-do list. This is who you are — 23 protocols, read daily, revised never (except on Sunday).

Read 15 min daily At 8:15 AM Same chair, same time
I

Identity Protocols

Protocols 1–5
01

I am a man of execution.

Planning is limited to the Sunday review; every other hour is action.

02

I never miss twice.

One broken day is data; two is a decision — and I don't make that decision.

03

I start with 2 minutes.

I am never required to finish — only to begin.

04

I do the hardest thing first, when my brain is freshest.

05

I don't hide my capabilities out of fear; I withhold them out of choice — and I know the difference.

II

Body Protocols

Protocols 6–10
06

I eat breakfast every single day.

Starving myself is self-sabotage wearing a disguise.

07

I train 4 days a week.

Twenty minutes counts. Zero doesn't.

08

I drink 3 liters of water.

Fatigue is usually thirst.

09

I sleep at 21:45.

The bed is for sleep, not for scrolling, not for hiding.

10

I go outside every day.

Sunlight is medication I don't need a prescription for.

III

Mind Protocols

Protocols 11–15
11

My phone charges in the kitchen.

It is a tool, not a limb.

12

Timers run my work blocks — not mood, not motivation.

13

The existential void is acknowledged, not argued with.

I nod at it and keep working.

14

Guilt during rest is forbidden.

Rest is scheduled. Scheduled rest is work.

15

I don't redesign my systems mid-week.

Frustration edits nothing; only Sunday does.

IV

People Protocols

Protocols 16–19
16

My fiancée gets my presence, not my leftovers.

18:00–21:00 is hers.

17

I call my parents weekly.

They get my voice while I still have theirs.

18

I don't argue with stubborn people.

I protect my peace and walk away richer.

19

I let it pass — my father's law.

One insult ignored is a week of peace earned.

V

Money Protocols

Protocols 20–22
20

$3,100 moves to IBKR monthly, same date, no debate, no market-timing.

21

I don't touch the principal.

The portfolio is a one-way door until 2046.

22

I buy nothing to impress anyone.

Every dollar is a brick in the 2050 house.

VI · The Prime Protocol Protocol 23 — everything above serves this one sentence

Get to the ship, climb the ranks, retire my parents, and build the quiet life — and it all depends on what I do before 9:30 AM today.
