Standing Orders

The 7 Rules

The meals are the fuel; these are the guardrails. Red-flagged rules are the load-bearing ones.

Rule 01 · Hard

Never skip breakfast

ADHD brain without fuel = the "lying in bed" spiral. Starving yourself is self-sabotage wearing a disguise.

Rule 02 · 💧

Water: 2.5–3L/day

Fill a 1L bottle 3×; keep it at the desk. Fatigue is usually thirst.

Rule 03 · 🥬

1 veg with dinner minimum

Frozen veg is fine — zero prep shame.

Rule 04 · 🍬☕

Sugar zero, coffee capped

Sugar drinks → zero. Coffee capped at 2, none after 2pm — protects the 21:45 sleep keystone.

Rule 05 · 🍳

Fried food: max 1×/week

Your current habit, kept. Realistic beats perfect.

Rule 06 · Hard

Sunday batch-cook

Daal, chopped veggies, etc. — Fri–Sun work-lunch containers prepped before the work weekend starts.

Rule 07 · 💊

Consider supplements

Whey (cheapest protein per $) and Vitamin D (Canadian winters) — discuss with doctor.

🍲

The keystone hour of the food weekSunday Batch-Cook

Fri–Sun are 12-hour Valid days running on survival mode — no starving is a schedule rule, and this is how it's kept. One session on Sunday fills the work-lunch containers, so the hardest days of the week require zero food decisions and zero cooking. The diet plan and the schedule meet right here.

Why It's Built This Way

Three Design Principles

Principle 01

Protein is the backbone

Every one of the five feeds carries protein — tofu, soya, whey, daal, yogurt, milk, nuts. 110–130g lands automatically if the template is simply followed.

Principle 02

Low friction or it won't happen

Low-cook-effort meals, frozen veg allowed, grab-and-go snacks, batch-cooked containers. The plan assumes an ADHD brain and removes every prep barrier it can.

Principle 03

Kept habits beat perfect rules

Fried food once a week stays. Rice and daal stay. Indian-friendly, cheap, familiar — a clean diet you'll still be on in 2031 beats a perfect one abandoned by October.
