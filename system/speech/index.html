04c · The Operating System

The Speech

Break glass when You're lying in bed at 10am. The plan feels pointless. The void whispers. Then — read this aloud.

Harshdeep. Get up.

Not because life has a meaning you've finally found. You've stared into that void longer than anyone you know, and you already know the answer: the universe owes you nothing, and it explains nothing. Fine. Then the meaning is the one you build — and you've already chosen it.

You chose her. The one who picked you when you had nothing, not for money, not for status, but for the inner world you show no one else. Every hour you waste in bed is an hour stolen from the house, the lawn, the life you promised her in 2050.

You chose your parents. Farmers in Sangrur who will one day be retired by their son's hands. Not by his plans. His hands.

You chose the sea. Six months on, six months off, tax-advantaged, AI-proof, unordinary — everything you demanded of life, it offered you. All it asks back is this year. One year of pre-study, one application, one clean exit from Salmon Arm.

You have ADHD. So what — now you know the enemy's name. It was never laziness. It was never weakness. Your brain needs structure, timers, and starts of two minutes — and you have all three written down, taped to your wall.

You are not the boy who plans and never executes. That story ends today, because you know exactly what your 16-year-old self needed to hear: a bad schedule executed beats a perfect one ignored.

The deer doesn't ask why the lion exists. It runs anyway.

So run. Two minutes. Start.
The rest will follow — it always does, once you start.

The speech ends in action

Don't close this page and think about it. Commit only to starting — momentum does the rest.
