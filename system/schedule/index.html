Why It's Built This Way

Three Load-Bearing Walls

Wall 01

Phone lives in the kitchen

It charges there overnight and stays there during work blocks. It is a tool, not a limb. The morning is won before the phone is touched.

Wall 02

Sleep at 21:45 is the keystone

The 6:30 wake, the workout, the fresh-brain study block — all of it collapses if the sleep time slips. This is the one block that is never negotiated.

Wall 03

Rest is scheduled, not stolen

Lunch rest, the 18:00–21:00 evening, survival-mode weekends — all deliberate. Scheduled rest is work. Guilt during rest is forbidden.
