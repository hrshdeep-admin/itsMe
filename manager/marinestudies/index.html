Nov 09–15, 2026

08 HRS⌄
LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Develop reliable numerical calculation skills and understand how engineering quantities and units must be handled.

01 / KNOWLEDGE

What you’ll study

Four carefully scoped units
01

Arithmetic foundations

  • Order of operations, signed numbers, fractions and decimal arithmetic
  • Convert between fractions and decimals; calculate percentages and percentage change
  • Simple engineering examples involving efficiency, losses and remaining quantities
02

Powers, roots & precision

  • Squares, cubes, square roots and integer exponents
  • Scientific notation for large and small quantities
  • Decimal places, significant figures and sensible numerical precision
03

Engineering measurement systems

  • SI base units: metre, kilogram, second and kelvin
  • Derived units: newton, pascal, joule and watt
  • Prefixes: milli, centi, kilo and mega
  • Unit conversion with cancellation and dimensional consistency
04

Common engineering conversions

  • Length, area, volume and mass conversions
  • Pa, kPa, MPa, bar and psi; introductory feet and inches
  • US versus Imperial gallons; compound conversions when factors are supplied
