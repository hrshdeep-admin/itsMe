Roadmap

Each week is named by its month and week number (Week 2 Oct covers Oct 8–14; week 4 of a month runs from the 22nd to month end). Every course is studied in order, from first principles to the harder material, and finished before the next one starts.

Daily plan, weeks 1–12

Four sessions a week, each with one topic to learn and one homework task. Tests sit on the last day of a course block. Tick sessions off as you go; ticks are saved in this browser only.

Every 2-hour session

  • 10 min recap of the last session
  • 65 min learn the new topic
  • 45 min homework

Notes

What this plan covers

Semester 1 and Semester 2 only. The full diploma has 3,009 classroom hours over 7 semesters, and 280 hours of self-study can only build the base. That base (maths, physics, chemistry, drawings, engine basics) is what the first two semesters lean on.

Each topic is a first pass. You are learning the vocabulary and the method, not mastering the course.

Where the topics come from

Course codes, names and classroom hours come from the college's 2027–2028 program outline. The detailed course outlines could not be read automatically, so the topic lists use the course titles and standard marine engineering fundamentals. When you can, look up each code in the course outline search and swap in any topic that differs.

Dates

Weeks run 1–7, 8–14, 15–21 and 22 to month end, four per month. The plan starts in Week 2 of October 2026 and ends in Week 4 of June 2027, which gives 35 study weeks. July is held as reserve.

Weeks 11 and 12 fall over the holidays. Skip them if you need to and use the July reserve to catch up.

Before September 2027

The program page says a valid Transport Canada marine medical, stating fit for sea service (with or without limitations), must be submitted before the program starts. Start that process early. Also check the college's laptop requirements.

Free study material

Khan Academy for algebra, trigonometry and physics. OpenStax for free College Physics and Chemistry textbooks. The college will name its own textbooks after you enrol; buy those rather than extras.
