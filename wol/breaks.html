Why this routine? Research & limits

The components have research support, but this exact 15-minute combination has not been tested as a package. It is a practical synthesis, not a guaranteed performance boost.

  • Micro-break meta-analysis — small improvements in energy and fatigue; no clear overall performance improvement.
  • CDC activity break guide — movement breaks are a practical way to interrupt sitting.
  • Five-minute breathing trial — regular guided breathing helped mood; effects for any single break are uncertain.
  • Nature exposure meta-analysis — modest, variable attention benefits; three minutes is not a proven dose.
  • If–then planning meta-analysis — linking a cue to one clear action improves follow-through.

Move gently. If slow breathing feels uncomfortable or dizzying, return to your natural breath. No special drink or outdoor space required. This app works offline; optional chimes and screen wake lock depend on your browser.
